Ownership & Orphan Handling

Protectarr remembers which application owned each download. When one disappears from that application's queue it becomes an orphan, and only then can the category fallback act on it.

How long a previously application-owned download must remain continuously confirmed absent before category fallback may act on it.

Confirmed absence means Protectarr successfully read that application's queue and the download was not in it. A queue it could not reach does not count and does not advance the clock - an application that is down or restarting never ages its own downloads into orphans. If the download reappears the clock resets to zero.

A download that has finished is never an orphan either: succeeding also removes it from the queue, which is the application importing it rather than abandoning it.
